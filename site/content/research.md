Every craftable set has one research project, in the mod's own research tab. Researching it unlocks both the armor and its helmet, and the prestige version when Royalty is loaded.

The glitter suit has no research project of its own. With Beyond Our Reach and Combat Extended both loaded, a glitter armor project appears that lets you craft the set.
