Each craftable tech level has a light, a medium and a heavy set. The heavier the set, the more it protects and the more it weighs.

- The armored suits are industrial gear made at a machining table from steel, plasteel, components and devilstrand.
- The advanced spacer sets are made at a fabrication bench and need advanced components, uranium and hyperweave.
- The glitter suit can't be crafted. Without Combat Extended it's sold by traders who carry high-tech armor and turns up as a quest reward. With Combat Extended, exotic and combat-supplier orbital traders sell it; with Royalty, Empire traders stock it too and Empire glitter guards wear it.

The prestige versions need Royalty. They protect a little less than the regular set and cost less to make.

Use the switch at the top of the page to see the stats with or without Combat Extended. Hover over an item's name for its in-game description.
