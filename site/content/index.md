## What the mod does

Armors For All Post-Medieval Tech Levels adds seven armor sets, each with a matching helmet, spread over three tech levels:

- <span class="t-industrial">Armored suits</span> (industrial): the light, combat and heavy-duty suits, made at a machining table.
- <span class="t-spacer">Advanced spacer</span>: flanker, vanguard and breacher armor, made at a fabrication bench.
- <span class="t-ultra">Glitter</span> (ultratech): the glitter suit. You buy it or earn it, or craft it if Beyond Our Reach is loaded.

Every craftable set has its own research project, and each tech level has a backpack. With Royalty, the heavy-duty, vanguard and glitter sets also come in prestige versions.

With Vanilla Expanded Framework, some of the armor grants abilities: the flanker armor can skip behind an enemy, the breacher armor fires an EMP burst, and the glitter suit has a warp pack and a sprint boost.

## What you need

RimWorld 1.6 or 1.7. No other mod is required. Combat Extended, Vanilla Expanded Framework, Royalty and Beyond Our Reach each add something when they're loaded; [Requirements & compatibility](requirements.html) lists what.
