## Inspirations

- The light suit is based on a light armor from [Menace](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2432860/MENACE/) by Overhype Studios.
- The combat suit is based on the base armor from [Helldivers 2](https://store.steampowered.com/app/553850/HELLDIVERS_2/) by Arrowhead Studios.
- The heavy-duty suit is based on a design from the movie Starship Troopers: Annihilation.
- The flanker and vanguard armor are based on armor from the Halo franchise.
- The breacher armor is based on designs from the Gloryhammer music video [Die With a Sword In My Hand](https://youtu.be/l7FdL1LArWo).
- The glitter set is an original design by Pesky.
- The armor abilities are inspired by [A RimWorld of Magic](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1201382956) by Torann, and by [JecsTools](https://github.com/jecrell/JecsTools) by jecrell, the ability framework it's built on.
